<Section title="The challenge">
Chanting and mantra counting are usually done with physical prayer beads, with no way to track
progress, join a chant with others remotely, or take part in a community event without being
physically present. There was no digital equivalent that felt respectful of the practice rather
than gimmicky.
</Section>

<Section title="The approach">
I designed a digital beads experience that mirrors the physical ritual — a bead-by-bead counter,
running totals, and round tracking — alongside voice chanting, groups, and live community events
tied to real occasions, like a nationwide chant for a royal anniversary. Sign-in through Bhutan's
National Digital ID keeps the whole experience grounded in a real, trusted identity.
</Section>

<Section title="The outcome">
A complete chanting companion covering digital beads, voice chant, groups, live events, and
personal progress reporting, built as one consistent product rather than a single counter screen.

- Designed a digital beads counter that mirrors the physical ritual, down to counts, rounds, and
  running totals.
- Built live community events around real occasions, with real-time chanting counts shown to
  participants.
- Added personal reporting — total mantras chanted, year-over-year progress — so practice has a
  visible record.
</Section>
