## Problem

The existing OneClickShop website lacked a clear structure, consistent branding, and a smooth
shopping experience. Users struggled with navigation, product discovery, and checkout flow,
leading to low engagement and poor conversion performance.

## What I did

I redesigned the entire OneClickShop website with a cleaner layout, improved product hierarchy,
and a more intuitive shopping journey. The revamp includes modern UI elements, simplified
navigation, stronger visual branding, and a mobile-first responsive design. The result is a
faster, clearer, and more user-friendly e-commerce experience that supports better browsing and
higher conversions.

## Approach

- Audited the existing site to find where navigation and product discovery broke down.
- Rebuilt the information hierarchy around product categories and search, not just visual style.
- Simplified the checkout flow to reduce steps and friction at the point of highest drop-off.
