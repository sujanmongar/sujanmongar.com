<Section title="The outcome">
A faster, clearer and more user-friendly e-commerce experience that supports better browsing and
higher conversions — covering search and autocomplete, filtered results, product detail, cart and
a three-step checkout, all responsive down to mobile.

- Audited the existing site to find where navigation and product discovery broke down.
- Rebuilt the information hierarchy around product categories and search, not just visual style.
- Simplified the checkout flow to reduce steps and friction at the point of highest drop-off.
</Section>
