<Section title="The challenge">
Bhutan doesn't have a matchmaking platform built for its own context — global dating apps
aren't designed around a small, close-knit population where trust, privacy, and verification
matter more than sheer volume. People looking to meet someone nearby had no local, trustworthy
option built for them.
</Section>

<Section title="The approach">
I designed a mobile-first matchmaking app around trust and simplicity: verified profiles, a
profile-completion score, straightforward swipe-based discovery, and a clear free-to-paid
subscription structure — with Events, Matches, Discover, Chats, and Profile built as one
consistent system rather than separate features bolted together.
</Section>

<Section title="The outcome">
A complete matchmaking product covering onboarding, discovery, profile verification, and
subscriptions, designed as one cohesive UI system rather than a set of disconnected screens.

- Designed a discovery flow built around trust signals — verification badges, profile-completion
  scoring, and location and zodiac details shown up front.
- Structured a clear free-to-paid subscription model, from unlimited swipes to unlimited chats.
- Kept Events, Matches, Discover, Chats, and Profile consistent as one navigation system.
</Section>
